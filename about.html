About Me

Loretta (Shu-Ling) Wang, EA

Loretta (Shu-Ling) Wang is an Enrolled Agent (EA) federally authorized by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to represent taxpayers across all administrative levels of the IRS.

Specializing in U.S.–Taiwan cross-border taxation, Loretta advises foreign professionals (L-1, H-1B, E-2), multinational executives, new permanent residents, and cross-border families on intricate dual-jurisdiction tax obligations. Her practice bridges U.S. federal and state compliance with cross-border asset structures, ensuring complete disclosure while avoiding severe foreign asset penalties.
